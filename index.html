    Capstone Project · NEMSU · 2026

    KitaWork

    A smart community employment system that connects job seekers with verified local employers in the CarCanMadCarLan area, so people can find work that fits their schedule.

    Visit the live site ↗
    23Job seekers
    11Employers
    10Active jobs

    Registered on the live site, October 2026

    The problem

    In small towns, finding work often depends on word of mouth, and it's hard for job seekers to know which employers are real. Employers, from businesses to households needing help, have no simple local place to find people. KitaWork brings both sides together in one place and checks everyone before they can take part.

    Who it's for

    Job Seekers

    Looking for part-time or full-time work nearby.

    Individual Hirers

    Households that need help with tasks around the home.

    Business Employers

    Registered companies hiring for part-time or full-time roles.

    How it works

    1. Pick a role. Job seeker, individual hirer or business employer.
    2. Add personal info, skills and schedule, so matches fit the hours people can actually work.
    3. Verify email and upload an ID. Businesses submit a business permit or DTI certificate.
    4. PESO reviews and approves the account. The local Public Employment Service Office checks every document.
    5. Job posts are reviewed too before they go public, keeping out fake or unsafe listings.

    What makes it different

    • Verified, not anonymous. Every user and job post is checked by PESO, so people can trust who they're dealing with.
    • Built around schedules. Skills and availability are part of sign-up, so people with limited hours can still find work.
    • Made for one community. Focused on the CarCanMadCarLan towns instead of being a generic job board.
    • Installable. It can be installed like an app straight from the browser.

    My role

    • Built it on my own. I designed and developed the whole system for my capstone, using AI coding tools to help me work faster.
    • Four kinds of users. Job seekers, hirers, employers and PESO staff each have their own dashboard, along with job matching, messaging and ID verification.
    • Presented and defended the system in front of a panel at NEMSU.
    • Built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and Supabase.
